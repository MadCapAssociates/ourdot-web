For guardians

Guardian consent form

No printer? Just write the note below by hand on any paper, sign and date it, and send a photo from OurDot (Family tab → Invite someone → Kid). Or print this page and sign it.

Updated October 24, 2026

The handwritten note (copy these words)

I am the parent or legal guardian of (your child’s first name). I have read OurDot’s Guardian Notice and agree to OurDot using my child’s information as it describes, only within our family.

Then add your signature, printed name and today’s date.
