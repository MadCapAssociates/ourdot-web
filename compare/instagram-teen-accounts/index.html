OurDot vs Instagram Teen Accounts

Instagram Teen Accounts add strong protections to a public network teens already use. OurDot is a private family space with no public side at all.

By the OurDot team · Updated October 24, 2026

In short

Instagram Teen Accounts (13+) default teens to private accounts, limited messaging, stricter content and a sleep mode, and under-16s need a parent's permission to loosen settings. It's still a public, ad-supported network with algorithmic feeds. OurDot is for families of every age, with no public profiles, no ads, no algorithm and no like counts.

What Teen Accounts do well

Strong defaults, announced in September 2024: private accounts, messaging only from people a teen follows, the strictest sensitive-content setting, Hidden Words, a 60-minute daily reminder and sleep mode. Under-16s need a parent's permission to change these. In 2025, Teen Accounts expanded to Facebook and Messenger, and Instagram added a PG-13-style "13+" content default. And teens' friends are already there.

Where they differ

Instagram is a public network built on algorithmic feeds and advertising, with a 13+ minimum. OurDot has no public side, no algorithm, no ads and no like counts, and it works for younger kids and grandparents too. Many families may use both: OurDot for family, and a supervised Teen Account when a teen is ready. See what age for social media.
