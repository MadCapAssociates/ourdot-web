OurDot vs Marco Polo

Marco Polo is a much-loved, ad-free way to send video messages across time zones. OurDot is built for whole families, including kids, with guardian controls.

By the OurDot team · Updated October 24, 2026

In short

Marco Polo's free core offers unlimited video messages with no ads, and a paid Plus plan adds more. Its terms set a minimum age of 13 and it has no guardian controls for kids. OurDot is free, works for kids of any age with a guardian, and adds a family feed, voice notes, live calls, games, lessons and meal planning.

Facts checked October 24, 2026. Apps change often; check each one's current terms.

What Marco Polo does well

Asynchronous video is wonderful for grandparents and families in different time zones: record when it suits you, watch when it suits them. It's ad-free, with no engagement-driven algorithm.

Where they differ

Marco Polo is designed for teens and adults. OurDot is designed for families with kids in them, with guardian roles and rules built in, and more ways to spend time together than video messages.
