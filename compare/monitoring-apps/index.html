OurDot vs monitoring apps like Bark and Qustodio

Monitoring apps watch for trouble across the apps kids already use. OurDot builds a family space where most of that trouble can't reach. They solve different problems, and many families may want both.

By the OurDot team · Updated October 24, 2026

In short

Monitoring apps like Bark and Qustodio scan, filter and limit across a child's phone, for a yearly fee. OurDot is free and preventive: a closed family space with rules built in. Monitoring is reactive; OurDot is a place to be. They work well together.

What monitoring apps do well

They work across the apps kids already use, which makes them useful for older kids on mainstream platforms. Bark, for example, sends alerts about risk signals without giving parents full access to every message.

Where they differ

Monitoring watches risky spaces; OurDot builds a calmer one. The UK's Children's Code encourages telling children when they're being monitored, and research on parenting suggests kids share more when they feel trusted, so OurDot shows patterns in a weekly summary rather than transcripts, and kids know what guardians can see.
