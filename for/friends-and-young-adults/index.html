Who it’s for

For friends, young adults and grown-ups without kids.

Want your people without everything else social media comes with? Start a private space for your friends, your partner, your roommates or your cousins. All of the connection, none of the addiction.

Updated November 4, 2026

In short

Anyone 18 or older can start a space for free and invite whoever they choose. You get a feed that ends, group chats, voice notes, video calls and games. There are no ads, no public like counts, no streaks and no algorithm deciding what you see.

What you get

  • A feed of your people, newest first, that ends with "You're all caught up"
  • Group chats, voice notes and video calls
  • Private hearts that only the poster sees
  • Games to play solo or together, like the daily word puzzle and trivia about each other
  • Shared meal plans and one shopping list in Family Kitchen
  • A private journal

What you don't get

  • Strangers, search or friend requests
  • Ads or outside tracking tools
  • Like counts, follower numbers or streaks
  • Endless scroll or autoplay
  • An algorithm picking what you see

Good to know

  • It's called a family, even if it's all friends. In the app, your private space is a "family," and the person who starts it is its guardian. That just means you can invite people and manage the space.
  • One space per person, for now. The app shows one OurDot family per person, so everyone you add shares one feed. Group chats let you talk with just some of them.
  • Under 18? Ask a parent or guardian to invite you. That's the only way anyone under 18 joins.

Why it's built this way

Endless feeds, autoplay and public like counts are among the design features health experts point to when they talk about apps built to keep people on them. OurDot leaves them out on purpose. Read what the research says about endless scroll and like counts, including where the evidence is still mixed.

Common questions

Can I use OurDot with just my friends?

Yes. Anyone 18 or older can start a space and invite friends. There don’t need to be any kids or relatives in it.

Is OurDot only for families?

No. The app calls your private space a family, but it can be friends, a couple, roommates, relatives or any mix.

Can my friends see who else I know on OurDot?

No. There are no friend lists or follower counts to see, and nobody outside your space can find you.
