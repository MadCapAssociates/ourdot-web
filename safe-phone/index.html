Coming to OurDot

A safe phone, from the phone you already have

Hand-me-down phone? Turn it into a calm OurDot phone. Only the apps you choose, no web unless you turn it on, and you’ll know right away if protection ever turns off.

Updated November 21, 2026

In short

The OurDot safe phone is a paid service, coming soon, that locks down a phone your family already owns. It isn’t a new phone and it isn’t a phone plan. It works with any carrier, or on Wi-Fi only. It isn’t available yet, and we won’t guess a date. The OurDot app itself stays free for everyone, always.

Only the apps you choose

OurDot and Phone stay open. Camera, Maps or anything else is on only if the guardian adds it. Everything else is blocked.

No web, unless you turn it on

The browser starts off at every age. A guardian can turn it on after reading why it’s off. Adults can turn it on for their own phone.

School time and sleep time

The phone follows the same times you already set in OurDot. The guardian line stays open, so your kid can always reach you.

You’ll know if it stops working

If protection turns off, the guardian gets an alert right away. A phone that hasn’t checked in for over a day is flagged too.

Who it’s for

Guardians who want their kid to have a phone for calls, photos and family messages, without the internet, app store and social media that come with a regular smartphone. It’s also for adults who want a calmer phone for themselves.

What phones it works on

  • iPhone: first. It uses Apple’s own Screen Time tools for families, through Apple Family Sharing. Apple has approved OurDot to use them.
  • Android: later. Locking an Android phone needs Google’s approval and a one-time reset of the phone during setup. We’ll share more once Google answers.
  • Phone plan or Wi-Fi only: both work. OurDot messages run over the internet, which helps when you’re traveling.

What we never see

OurDot never sees which apps your kid opens, for how long, or what websites they visit. On iPhone, Apple keeps the app choices and usage on the phone itself. OurDot’s servers hear only two things from a safe phone: whether it’s protected, and when it last checked in.

About 911

OurDot can’t call or text 911. An iPhone with no phone plan can usually still call 911 on any cell tower in range, but never over Wi-Fi. A device with no cellular service at all, like a Wi-Fi iPad, can’t call 911. When a safe phone may not reach 911, a warning stays at the top of OurDot on that phone, and the setup guide ends with a talk about what to do in an emergency.

How setup will work (iPhone)

About 15 minutes, with both phones and your Apple Account password. Each step will have a short video, written steps and a picture.

  1. Your kid signs in with your email, plus their name

    Like sam+leo@gmail.com. Every sign-in code comes to your inbox, so your kid can’t sign in on a new phone without you.

  2. Give your kid an Apple Child Account in your Family

    On your iPhone: Settings, then Family, then Add Member, then Create Child Account. Apple requires this before any app can lock a phone.

  3. Sign the phone in to your kid’s Apple Account

    If the phone used to be yours, sign out of your Apple Account first.

  4. Put OurDot on the phone

    It’s free. Sign in as your kid with the +name email.

  5. Turn on the safe phone

    In OurDot on your kid’s phone: Family, then Safe phone, then Turn on this phone. Apple asks you, the guardian, to approve.

  6. Choose the extra apps

    OurDot and Phone stay open. Tick Camera and any extra apps you want. Everything else is blocked.

  7. Check that it worked

    On your own phone, your kid should show “Protected.”

  8. Talk with your kid about 911

    OurDot can’t call 911. Show your kid the phone’s own Emergency call.

What it will cost

A small monthly fee for the safe phone service, with a lower price for families facing financial hardship. You’ll see the price before you sign up, and you can cancel any time. The OurDot app, and everything in it, stays free for everyone.

The honest limits

  • No phone protection is perfect. Apple has a known issue where a child can sometimes remove a protected app. That’s why OurDot shows you a live status and alerts you if protection turns off.
  • Apple and Google decide what any app can lock on their phones. If they change their rules, we’ll tell you plainly what changed.
  • The safe phone doesn’t replace talking with your kid. The setup guide includes the conversations that matter most.

Read the safe phone service terms. Questions? help@ourdot.app.

Common questions

Is the OurDot safe phone available now?

Not yet. We’ll announce it in the OurDot app when it’s ready. We won’t guess a date.

Do I need to buy a new phone?

No. That’s the point. It works on a phone your family already owns. iPhone comes first; Android comes later.

Does it work without a phone plan?

Yes. It works on Wi-Fi only. Without a plan, though, 911 may not work, and the phone shows a warning that says so.

Can my kid delete OurDot or turn it off?

On iPhone, Apple’s Screen Time stops a child deleting OurDot without a guardian’s approval. No protection is perfect, so if it ever turns off, you get an alert right away.

Does OurDot see what my kid does on the phone?

No. OurDot never sees which apps are opened, for how long, or which websites are visited. It only knows whether the phone is protected and when it last checked in.

Is OurDot still free?

Yes. The OurDot app is free for everyone, always. Only the optional safe phone service will have a monthly fee, with a hardship price.
