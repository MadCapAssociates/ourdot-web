Safe phone service terms

Updated November 21, 2026

The short version

The safe phone service helps a guardian lock down a phone their family already owns. It isn’t an emergency service, it can’t promise a phone can never be gotten around, and it never sees what apps or websites someone uses. These terms take effect when the service opens. Until then, nothing is sold.

1. Who we are

The OurDot safe phone service is provided by MadCap Associates LLC, 100 N Howard St Pmb R, Spokane, WA 99201 (“OurDot,” “we”). help@ourdot.app · 737-252-6845. These terms add to the OurDot terms of use, which also apply.

2. What the service is

Software that uses the phone maker’s own tools to limit a phone to the apps a guardian (or an adult, for their own phone) chooses, block the web unless it’s turned on, follow OurDot’s school time and sleep time, and show whether the phone is protected. It isn’t a phone, a phone plan, or a carrier. On iPhone, it uses Apple’s Screen Time tools through Apple Family Sharing. Android support depends on Google’s approval.

3. Who can use it

  • A guardian 18 or older, for a child they’re the parent or legal guardian of, on a phone the guardian owns or has the right to manage.
  • An adult, for their own phone.
  • On iPhone, a child’s phone must be signed in to a Child Account in the guardian’s Apple Family Sharing group.

4. What you agree to do

  • Give accurate information, including whether the phone has a phone plan.
  • Follow the setup steps, and check the phone shows “Protected.”
  • Keep your own Apple Account (or Google Account) password private. It’s what lets you approve changes.
  • Talk with your child about safe phone use and what to do in an emergency.

5. Not an emergency service

OurDot can’t call or text 911 or any emergency number. Whether a phone can reach 911 depends on the phone, its plan and cell coverage, not on OurDot. A phone with no plan may reach 911 only where a cell tower is in range, and never over Wi-Fi. A device with no cellular service can’t call 911. When a safe phone may not reach 911, OurDot shows a warning on it, but you’re responsible for knowing how your child can get help.

6. What the service can’t promise

  • No phone protection is perfect. Apple and Google control what can be locked, their tools have known issues (for example, a child sometimes removing a protected app), and they can change their rules.
  • We alert the guardian when a phone reports that protection turned off, and flag a phone that hasn’t checked in for over a day. A phone that’s off, offline or reset can’t report.
  • The service is provided as is, to the extent the law allows. It’s one layer of safety, not a replacement for a guardian’s care.

7. What we collect for the service

  • Settings: whether the browser is on (and when the warnings were accepted), the names of extra apps the guardian plans to allow, and whether the phone has a phone plan.
  • Status: each safe phone’s label (like “Leo’s phone”), whether it’s an iPhone or Android phone, whether it’s protected, a short reason if it isn’t, and when it last checked in.
  • Alerts: when protection turned off or came back on.
  • Never: which apps are opened, how long, websites visited, messages outside OurDot, or location. On iPhone, the guardian’s app choices stay on the phone with Apple.

Only the person and their guardians can see this. A guardian can remove an old phone from the list any time. Deleting the OurDot account deletes all of it. Children’s information is handled as described in the guardian notice and privacy policy.

8. Fees

The OurDot app is free for everyone, always. The safe phone service will have a monthly fee, with a lower hardship price. You’ll see the exact price and how you’re billed before you sign up, and you won’t be charged until you do. You can cancel any time; you keep the service until the end of the period you paid for.

9. Turning it off

A guardian can turn the safe phone off on the phone, in the phone’s Screen Time settings with their approval, and cancel the service any time. When a phone reports that protection turned off, its guardians get an alert, by design.

10. Changes and the law

If these terms change in a way that matters, we’ll tell you in the app before the change takes effect. These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Washington, USA.
