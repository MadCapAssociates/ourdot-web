Get started

Start your space, free.

Anyone 18 or older can set one up on the web in about two minutes, for friends, family or both. Then you invite the people you choose. Nobody else can find you or join.

Starting your space

  1. Open ourdot.app/app

    On any phone, tablet or computer. Nothing to install.

  2. Enter your email

    We send a 6-digit code. It's in the subject line, so you can read it from the notification. There's no password to remember.

  3. Choose "Start a family"

    In OurDot, your private space is called a family, even if it's all friends. Give it a name and add yours. If there will be kids, add what they call you (like Mom, Dad or Grandma Jo).

  4. Confirm you're 18 or older

    If you'll invite kids, you also confirm you're their parent or legal guardian and agree to the beta consent.

  5. Invite your people

    From the Family tab: friends, relatives, a co-guardian and, if you're their guardian, your kids. Each gets a link to tap. Before your first kid invite, you'll handwrite and sign a short consent note on any paper and send a photo of it (a children's privacy law requirement, done once).

Joining a space someone else started?

Tap the invite link they sent you. Sign in with your email, and the invite fills in for you. If it asks, choose "I have an invite code."

Open my invite

Setting up a child

Kids don't need their own email. Use yours with a plus sign and their name, like you+leo@gmail.com. Their sign-in codes land in your inbox, and OurDot never stores a child's own email.

Works with Gmail, Yahoo, Proton and Fastmail. iCloud Mail doesn't support plus addresses, so use another email you check.

Who you can invite

  • Friends and relatives 18 and older, anyone you choose.
  • Kids, if you're their guardian, with an age range: under 13, 13 to 15, or 16 to 17.
  • Co-guardians, who get the same controls you have.
  • Around kids, every adult who isn't a guardian follows the trusted-adult rules: they see posts and the group chat, but never message a child one-on-one.

For now, the app shows one OurDot family per person, so everyone you add shares one feed. Group chats let you talk with just some of them.

Under 18?

Ask a parent or guardian to invite you. That's the only way anyone under 18 joins OurDot.

Common questions

Does it cost anything?

No. OurDot is free for everyone, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Can I create an account without an invite?

Yes, if you’re 18 or older. You can start your own space for friends, family or both. Anyone under 18 joins only through a parent or guardian’s invite.

Do I need kids to use OurDot?

No. Start a space with your friends, your partner, your roommates or your relatives. The kid protections are built in for any guardian who adds a child later.

Do I need to download anything?

No. OurDot works in any web browser at ourdot.app/app. The iPhone app is on its way to the App Store, and you can add the web version to your home screen today.

Can I use OurDot on a computer?

Yes. Sign in at ourdot.app/app from any computer. Your family, messages and settings are the same everywhere you sign in.
