Open OurDot

Put OurDot on your home screen today

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open ourdot.app/app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open ourdot.app/app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.

The icon opens OurDot full screen, like any app. When the store apps arrive, sign in with the same email and everything will be there.

Common questions

Is OurDot on the App Store?

Not yet. The iPhone app is being prepared for Apple’s review. The web version works on iPhone today, and you can add it to your home screen.

Is there an Android app?

Google Play comes after the App Store. Android phones can use OurDot in Chrome now and install it to the home screen.

Will my family and messages carry over to the app?

Yes. Everything lives in your OurDot account, not on one device. Sign in with the same email on any device.
