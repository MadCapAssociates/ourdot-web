Child safety

OurDot child safety standards

Updated November 3, 2026

The short version

OurDot, the private family app from MadCap Associates LLC, has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We prohibit it, make it easy to report, remove it, report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), and close the accounts involved.

1. What we prohibit

On OurDot, no one may create, share, request or link to child sexual abuse material (CSAM); sexualize a minor in any way, including drawings, edited or computer-generated images, text or voice; groom, sexually solicit or sextort a child; or use OurDot to arrange contact with a child for sexual purposes or to traffic a child. This applies to every account, every family and every feature: posts, messages, photos, video and voice notes, calls, channels, games, wish lists and the marketplace. It is also part of our terms of use.

2. How OurDot is built to prevent it

  • Families are invite-only. There are no public profiles, no search for strangers and no friend requests.
  • Kids join only with a guardian's invite and signed consent, and guardians approve everyone in the family.
  • Adults who aren't a child's guardian never message that child one-on-one, and a child is never on a call alone with an adult who isn't their guardian.
  • An on-phone check pauses unkind words and blocks explicit words in chats that include kids.
  • Kids never see the marketplace or ads, and adults can't advertise to anyone under 18.

3. How to report

Anyone can report from inside the app. Tap ••• on any post, or press and hold any message, then choose Report. Pick "Sexual content, or a child is at risk." These reports are urgent. The post or message is hidden right away, and a real person on the OurDot team reviews it. You can also block the person from the same menu, and a child's report can be kept private from everyone except the OurDot team.

You can also email help@ourdot.app. If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 first. You can report child sexual exploitation directly to NCMEC at CyberTipline.org or 1-800-843-5678.

4. How we handle CSAM

  • Reported content is hidden from everyone as soon as it's reported.
  • Our reviewers report apparent CSAM to NCMEC's CyberTipline, as US law requires (18 U.S.C. § 2258A).
  • We keep the reported material locked and hidden for law enforcement for as long as the law requires, and we never show it to other users.
  • We remove the content and suspend or close the accounts involved.
  • Reviewers are told not to view or forward the material more than is needed to report it.

5. Laws we follow

We comply with child safety laws that apply to OurDot, including US federal law on reporting and preserving CSAM, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the TAKE IT DOWN Act, which requires removing non-consensual intimate images within 48 hours of a valid request. We cooperate with law enforcement requests made through proper legal process.

6. Child safety contact

Our designated child safety point of contact is Taraya Voelker, MadCap Associates LLC, the developer of OurDot. Email help@ourdot.app with "Child safety" in the subject line, or call 425-533-6676.
