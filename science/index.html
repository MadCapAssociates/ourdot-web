The science

Every feature starts with the research.

Plain-English summaries of what studies and health organizations actually found, including where the evidence is mixed and what critics say.

Updated October 24, 2026

How we use research

We read the strongest sources we can find, like meta-analyses and guidance from pediatric and psychological associations, and we say how strong the evidence is. Many findings are associations, not proof of cause. When experts disagree, we show both sides.
